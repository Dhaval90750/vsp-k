# Proposals

Quotes and proposals sent to clients.

## How to write one

1. Copy [proposal-template.md](proposal-template.md) to `proposals/YYYY-MM-DD-<client-name>.md`.
2. Fill it in from the signed brief (`clients/<client>/01-brief.md`).
3. Export it to PDF and send it within 24 hours of the call.
4. When the client accepts, copy the final version into `clients/<client>/02-quote.md`.

## Rules

- Fixed price and fixed date, always.
- List what's **not** included as clearly as what is.
- Changes after acceptance get their own price and date (see the change log in the client folder).
