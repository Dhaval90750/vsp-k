How we work

Five steps. No skipping. No surprises.

  1. 1

    Talk

    A 15-minute call or a WhatsApp chat. We write down what you need on one page, in plain words.

  2. 2

    Quote

    A fixed price and a fixed date, in writing. No “depends”, no hourly meter running.

  3. 3

    Build

    You get a working link to check every few days. Real software you can click, not slides.

  4. 4

    Prove

    Tested on a real budget Android phone and an iPhone. Speed and accessibility checked before you see “done”.

  5. 5

    Hand over

    Live site, all logins, a short “how to edit” guide, and a note listing exactly what is done and what isn't.
