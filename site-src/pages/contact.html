It depends on what you need, so we don't publish one price that fits nobody. Tell us what you need and you'll get a fixed quote within 24 hours. The price you see is the price you pay.

Yes. The domain, hosting, code and every account are in your name. If you ever want to move to someone else, you can take everything with you.

Who writes the text and provides photos?

You know your business best, so you give us the facts. We turn them into clear, short text and help you plan simple photos with your phone. We never use fake reviews or stock photos pretending to be your team.

Small fixes for issues we caused are free for the period stated in your quote. After that you can pick a monthly maintenance plan or pay per change, always quoted before we start.
