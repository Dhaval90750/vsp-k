# LinkedIn

LinkedIn uses the same 1080 × 1350 images as Instagram (from `../instagram/`). The difference is the caption: LinkedIn versions are "joke + business lesson" and are in [../captions.md](../captions.md).

## Tips

- Post as a **document (PDF)** for carousels: combine the carousel PNGs into one PDF and upload it as a document. It swipes like Instagram.
- Best times: weekday mornings, 8–10 am IST.
- Keep 3 hashtags at most.
- Reply to every comment within the first hour.
