# VSp!k Partner Programme

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Version | 1.0 |
| Date | 2026-11-15 |

## Tiers

| Tier | Who | What they do | Commission |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Referrer** | Anyone | Shares a contact. VSp!k does the sales, building and support | **10%** of the first project |
| **Partner** | After 3 paid referrals | Makes a warm introduction and joins the first call | **15%** of the first project + **5%** of maintenance renewals for 12 months |
| **White-label** | Agencies | Sells under their own brand; VSp!k builds in the background | Fixed partner price. The agency sets its own margin |

## Rules

1. Commission is paid **only after the client has paid VSp!k**, within **7 days**.
2. **Referral credit lasts 90 days** from the introduction. If the client signs within 90 days, the referrer gets paid.
3. "First project" means the first signed quote, excluding hosting, domains, third-party fees and taxes.
4. Partners cannot promise prices, dates or features on VSp!k's behalf.
5. If a client gets a refund, the commission on that amount is returned or adjusted.
6. Client details shared by partners stay confidential.
7. GST and TDS are handled as required. Confirm the set-up with a CA.

## How to track

Keep a simple sheet with these columns: partner, client, introduction date, quote amount, client paid date, commission %, commission amount, paid date.

## Marketing rules

- "Earn 10%" and "up to 15% + 5%" are fine because they are the real terms.
- Don't publish total payouts ("partners earned ₹5 lakh") until it's true and checkable (Quality Framework §7).
