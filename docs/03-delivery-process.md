# Delivery process

From the first message to handover. Every client project follows these steps (Quality Framework §2).

| # | Step | What happens | Output | File |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Enquiry** | Client messages on WhatsApp, phone or email. Reply the same day | Short call booked | — |
| 2 | **Brief** | 15-minute call. Write down the goal, users, pages/features, content owner and deadline | Signed one-page brief | [`clients/_template/01-brief.md`](../clients/_template/01-brief.md) |
| 3 | **Quote** | Fixed price, fixed date, scope, payment terms. Sent within 24 hours | Accepted quote | [`proposals/proposal-template.md`](../proposals/proposal-template.md) |
| 4 | **Advance** | Advance payment received (suggested 50%) | Project starts | — |
| 5 | **Build** | Start from a starter in `templates/`. Share a working link every 2–3 days | Working preview link | — |
| 6 | **Changes** | Any change outside the brief gets its own price and date before work starts | Change log | [`clients/_template/04-change-log.md`](../clients/_template/04-change-log.md) |
| 7 | **Prove** | Test on a real Android phone and an iPhone at 360/768/1280 px. Lighthouse, accessibility, link check | Evidence (screenshots, scores) | — |
| 8 | **Deliver** | Go live. Hand over logins, a short "how to edit" guide and the delivery note | Delivery note | [`clients/_template/03-delivery-note.md`](../clients/_template/03-delivery-note.md) |
| 9 | **Final payment** | Balance received | Project closed | — |
| 10 | **Follow-up** | After 2 weeks: check in, ask for a review or testimonial (real ones only), and offer maintenance | Case study (with permission) | [`portfolio/case-study-template.md`](../portfolio/case-study-template.md) |
